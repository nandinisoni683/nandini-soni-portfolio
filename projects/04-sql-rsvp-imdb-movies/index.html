RSVP Movies: IMDb SQL Case Study

Done at: upGrad Business Analyst Program (NSDC) · Jul 2024Tools: MySQL

Problem
RSVP Movies, an Indian production house, plans a film for a global audience. Which genre, director and cast give it the best chance?
Result
Drama is the most-made genre and Thriller is top 3. James Mangold stands out as a director, Marvel Studios gets the most votes, and Taapsee Pannu is the top-rated Hindi-film actress.

How I did it

Step by step

  1. Explored the data.Counted rows in each table and found which columns have missing values.
  2. Looked at release trends.Counted films per year and month, and per country.
  3. Analysed genres and ratings.Found the biggest genres, the top-rated films and the studios with the most hits.
  4. Analysed people.Ranked directors, actors and actresses by the ratings of their films.
  5. Wrote recommendationsfor RSVP Movies on genre, director, cast and production partner.

Data model

The six tables

Every film sits in the movie table. The other tables link to it through movie_id, and people link through name_id.

movie

One row per film: title, year, length, country and income. Every other table links to this one.

id, title, year, duration, country, production_company

ratings

Average rating, total votes and median rating for each film.

movie_id, avg_rating, total_votes, median_rating

genre

The genres of each film. A film can have several.

movie_id, genre

names

People: actors, actresses and directors.

id, name, date_of_birth

role_mapping

Who acted in which film, and as actor or actress.

movie_id, name_id, category

director_mapping

Who directed which film.

movie_id, name_id

SQL

Three example queries

A short sample of the 24 business questions I answered.

Which genre is made most?

Counts films per genre and keeps the top one. Answer: Drama.

select genre, count(movie_id) as movie_count
from genre
group by genre
order by movie_count desc
limit 1;

Top 10 films by rating

Joins films to their ratings and ranks them with a window function.

select m.title, avg_rating,
       row_number() over (order by avg_rating desc) as movie_rank
from movie as m
left join ratings as r on m.id = r.movie_id
order by avg_rating desc
limit 10;

Which studio makes the most hits?

A CTE counts films rated above 8 per production house, then RANK() picks the leader.

with top_prod as (
  select m.production_company, count(m.id) as movie_count,
         rank() over (order by count(m.id) desc) as prod_company_rank
  from movie as m
  left join ratings as r on m.id = r.movie_id
  where avg_rating > 8 and m.production_company is not null
  group by m.production_company
)
select * from top_prod where prod_company_rank = 1;
