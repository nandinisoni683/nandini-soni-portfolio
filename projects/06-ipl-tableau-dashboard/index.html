Project

IPL Performance Analysis

Done at: upGrad Business Analyst Program (NSDC) · 2024Tools: Tableau

Problem
Turn ball-by-ball and match data from the Indian Premier League into dashboards that show what wins matches, and who the standout teams and players are.
Result
Teams that won the toss and chose to field won 196 matches, against 124 when they chose to bat. Mumbai Indians hold the biggest win by runs (146) and Chennai Super Kings by wickets (10). SK Raina (4,548) and V Kohli (4,423) top the run charts, and SL Malinga leads the wicket-takers.
StepsMethodsDashboardsCharts

How I did it

Step by step

  1. Connected the data.Linked the match table and the ball-by-ball table in Tableau so every delivery knows its match, season and venue.
  2. Created new fields.Built calculated fields such as whether the toss winner also won the match, win vs. loss per season and home vs. away win %.
  3. Built one sheet per question.Toss impact, biggest wins, highest totals, top batsmen, top bowlers, season results and home vs. away.
  4. Filtered to what matters.Used Top 10 filters, a season (year) context filter and excluded run-outs from bowler wickets.
  5. Combined them into dashboards.Grouped the sheets into Match, Player and Team dashboards, with clicks on one chart filtering the others.

Formulas and methods

What I used

Data preparation

  • Joined the matches table to the ball-by-ball table on match ID
  • Combined match ID and innings into one key for team totals per innings

Calculated fields

  • Toss vs. Match Outcome: did the toss winner also win the match?
  • Win vs. Loss: +1 for a win and −1 for a loss, summed per team per season
  • Win % = matches won ÷ matches played, split by Home vs. Away

Filters

  • Top N filters to keep the top 10 batsmen, bowlers and teams
  • Context filter on season year so Top N respects the selected season
  • Dismissal kind filter that excludes run outs, retired hurt and obstructing the field from bowler wickets

Charts and dashboards

  • Bar charts, treemaps, packed bubbles, a diverging win/loss bar and a dot plot
  • Three dashboards with action filters, so clicking one chart filters the rest

Dashboards

The three dashboards

  • Match Statistics: toss impact, biggest wins and highest totals
  • Player Statistics: top run-scorers, big hitters and wicket-takers
  • Team Statistics: season-wise results and home vs. away win %
