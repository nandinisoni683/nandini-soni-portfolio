Project

SaaS Sales Pipeline Conversion

Done at: upGrad Business Analyst Program (NSDC) · Sep 2024Tools: Excel, PowerPoint

Problem
TechnoServe, a tech SaaS startup, saw its sales pipeline conversion fall from 35% to 25% in a year.
Result
Focus new leads on ERP Implementation deals, the Enterprise Sellers channel, smaller clients, deals of 0–30K and existing customers. These segments convert above average.
StepsPresentation

How I did it

Step by step

  1. Defined the problem.Wrote down who is affected, what changed, when, where and why, then listed the questions to ask the sales team.
  2. Listed every possible cause.Built an issue tree using the 5C framework (Customer, Company, Competition, Collaborators, Context), giving 18 branches of hypotheses.
  3. Ranked the causes.Gave each hypothesis a priority from P0 (most likely) to P4.
  4. Checked them against the data.Compared won vs. lost opportunities by solution type, sales channel, client size, deal size and client history in Excel.
  5. Turned insights into actions.Picked the five segments that convert best and presented them as recommendations.
