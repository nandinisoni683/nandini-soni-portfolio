# Sales Pipeline Conversion at a SaaS Startup

**Type:** Business analysis case study · **Tools:** Excel, PowerPoint · **Program:** upGrad Business Analyst

## Problem
TechnoServe, a tech SaaS startup, saw its sales pipeline conversion fall from **35% (end of FY 2017-18) to 25%**. The task was to understand the problem, form hypotheses for the drop, and analyse the opportunity data to recommend how to raise conversion.

## Approach
1. **Problem definition:** framed the problem with 5W1H (who, what, when, where, how) and drafted stakeholder questions using the SPIN method (Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff).
2. **Hypotheses:** built an **issue tree on top of the 5C framework** (Customer, Company, Competition, Collaborators, Context). This produced 18 branches, each with hypotheses ranked P0–P4 by priority.
3. **Analysis:** analysed the opportunity dataset in Excel by solution type, B2B sales channel, client revenue size, opportunity size and existing vs. new clients.
4. **Presentation:** turned the insights into recommendations for the sales team.

## Key findings
- **ERP Implementation** produces an unusually large share of opportunities and converts better than average.
- **Enterprise Sellers** convert better than average among B2B sales channels.
- Most opportunities come from clients with **revenue of 100K or less**.
- Conversion is significantly higher for **opportunities sized 0–30K**.
- **Existing customers** convert better, even though most opportunities come from new ones.

## Recommendation
Prioritise new leads that match the high-converting profile: ERP Implementation solutions, the Enterprise Sellers channel, smaller clients and deals, and existing customers.
