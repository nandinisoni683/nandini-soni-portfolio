# Restaurant Best-Selling Menu Analysis

**Type:** Mentor-led data analyst internship · **Tools:** Tableau, Excel  
**Done at:** Data Analyst internship, Mentormind × The Thickshake Factory · Sep–Oct 2024

## Problem
Help a restaurant improve its menu and grow sales by answering four questions:
1. What is the top-selling product?
2. How do sales move over time?
3. How do customers order (dine-in vs. takeaway)?
4. Which items sell together and could become combo meals?

## Approach
- Cleaned the raw sales data in Excel.
- Built Tableau views covering top products, the daily sales trend, order type split and a market basket analysis.
- Wrote a report with recommendations, assumptions, limitations and next steps.

## Key findings
- **Pizza Dosa** is the top-selling item.
- Daily sales fell from **8,310 units (6 Mar) to 920 (16 Mar)**, with a brief recovery around 17 Mar.
- **96.25% of orders are dine-in**, which leaves room to grow takeaway.
- **American Chopsuey** and **Pizza Dosa** are the most frequently paired items (13.79% each), making them a natural combo meal.

## Recommendations
- Promote a Pizza Dosa + American Chopsuey combo.
- Run mid-week promotions to counter the falling sales trend.
- Drop low sellers such as Plain Dosa.
- Improve the dine-in experience and promote takeaway.
