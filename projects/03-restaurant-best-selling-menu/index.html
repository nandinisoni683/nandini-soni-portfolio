Project

Restaurant Best-Selling Menu

Done at: Data Analyst internship, Mentormind × The Thickshake Factory · Sep–Oct 2024Tools: Tableau, Excel

Problem
Use the restaurant’s sales data to decide what belongs on a best-selling menu and which items to sell as a combo.
Result
Pizza Dosa is the top seller and 96% of orders are dine-in. Pizza Dosa and American Chopsuey are ordered together most often, so I recommended them as a combo meal, plus mid-week offers to lift falling sales.
StepsPresentationReportCase study

How I did it

Step by step

  1. Cleaned the sales datain Excel so every order had a product, date and order type.
  2. Found the top sellers.Built a Tableau chart ranking every item by units sold.
  3. Tracked sales over time.Plotted daily sales, which slid from 8,310 units on 6 March to 920 on 16 March.
  4. Checked how people order.Split orders into dine-in and takeaway.
  5. Found items bought together.Ran a market basket analysis to see which dishes appear in the same order.
  6. Wrote the recommendationsin a report with assumptions, limits and next steps.
