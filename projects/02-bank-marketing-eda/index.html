Project

Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis

Done at: upGrad Business Analyst Program (NSDC) · Jul 2024Tools: Advanced Excel (pivot tables, charts)

Problem
A bank ran a phone campaign to sell term deposits. Which customers should it target next time?
Result
Only 11.4% of customers signed up. The typical customer is 30–50, married, in a blue-collar or management job and reached on mobile, and activity peaks in May.
StepsKey numbersPresentation

How I did it

Step by step

  1. Cleaned the data.Removed duplicates and blank rows from 45,000+ records, fixed negative ages and inconsistent job labels.
  2. Prepared it for analysis.Turned yes/no answers into 1/0, split dates into day and month, and converted call time into seconds.
  3. Looked at one variable at a time.Used pivot tables and charts to see the spread of age, job, balance, loans, contact method and month.
  4. Compared variables together.Checked how pairs relate, for example age vs. balance and contact method vs. outcome.
  5. Summarised who to target.Wrote an observation on every chart and pulled out the five main insights.
